# Business analytics reports / 中英文商业分析报告

`report.tex` turns the executed EDA into an English business report: executive brief, data and questions, findings, a reproducible candidate pair, decision workflow, proposed business validation, scope, reproduction and references.

```powershell
./scripts/run_step4.ps1
./scripts/build_report.ps1
```

Current outputs: `output/food-value-report-en.pdf` (English) and `output/food-value-report-zh.pdf` (中文), each with an illustrated cover. Chinese source: `report_zh.tex`; Chinese charts: `outputs/step4/figures/zh/`, generated by `scripts/build_report_figures_zh.R` from the same executed analysis objects. Text, numerical tables, chart labels and captions are localised; original product names and bibliographic titles are preserved. `-Language en` / `zh` builds just one edition. Previous PDFs remain available locally. The build requires Python 3 (standard library only), XeLaTeX and installed Tau-class dependencies. Set `-Python` to another interpreter, or `-OutputName` with a single `-Language` for another filename within `output/`. `-RPrefix` selects the R environment used for Chinese figures. Tables are generated directly from executed CSV outputs, with primary/joint sample assertions. Build intermediates go to `scratch/report-build/`.

Template selection: the user's `C:/Users/VIP/latex-templates/` contains ElegantBook, kaobook and Tau-class. Tau's compact English article layout suits a decision report with findings and exhibits. We use a single column and green palette. Vendored Tau-class 2.6.0 is by Guillermo Jimenez under **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), original repository https://github.com/MemoJimenez/Tau-class. Local class changes remove unused minted and biblatex dependencies; the report uses an explicit bibliography and no shell execution. Other template files retain their upstream headers.

The numerical results are executed EDA. Commercial benefits are proposed and have not been measured in a user or retailer trial. This business report is ready for team review; final course assembly still needs named contributions, individual reflections and classroom feedback.

The visual revision adds a full-page editorial cover and an industry-background page. Both pictures are generated illustrations, labelled in the document; they do not depict sampled products. The original PNGs and exact prompts are in `assets/`, with generation mode and model-interface limitations recorded in `assets/provenance.json`. The shopping illustration is also served locally in the Shiny overview.

The publication revision removes section-by-section page breaks and fixed-position figure blocks. Text and floats now share pages naturally; the short business-question callout stays together. A measure/meaning/action table and a consumer/supplier decision section explain the value of the findings. Suppliers use retail-positioning and product-attribute benchmarks; costs, demand, margins and current commercial terms require additional evidence.

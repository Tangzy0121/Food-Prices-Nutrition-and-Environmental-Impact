# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Course reviewers, classmates and project collaborators exploring a food-price research project on laptop screens during a presentation or independently afterward.

## Product Purpose

Present the research problem and explain the real data-processing workflow with inspectable stages and source tables. R remains the analytical and course-delivery environment. This TypeScript site is a presentation layer. The website also provides a native TypeScript research dashboard organized around the user-confirmed six analytical directions: data coverage and price distribution, nutrition, environment, joint comparison, sensitivity, and paired year-to-year changes. Shiny remains a course deliverable.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, direct. Continue the Food / Signal project identity with the user's newly specified monochrome treatment.

## Anti-references

Avoid promotional slogans, stacks of secondary headings, small explanatory captions, decorative metric cards, excessive green, and generic SaaS landing-page patterns.

## Design Principles

- Let alignment, spacing and typography explain the structure.
- Make each transformation inspectable and keep record counts distinct from product counts.
- Use the whole pipeline page as a continuous canvas. Group related operations into readable nodes, preserving external dependencies. Let users inspect every underlying operation, its actual before/after records and R source provenance in the bottom data panel.
- Present committed R outputs; do not imply the browser executes the pipeline.
- Connect study, data pipeline and the six-direction research dashboard through the shared navigation. The chart plan in docs/05_dashboard/research_dashboard.md governs the dashboard; the older consumer-oriented Shiny interface is not its design reference.
- Support English and Simplified Chinese throughout the presentation, while preserving source code, product names and data keys.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls and readable text. Respect reduced motion. Provide a list alternative to the flow canvas and structurally responsive layouts.
